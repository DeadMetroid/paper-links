# VIDEO GUIDE — a single unbroken take

No cuts, no edits, no speed-ups. Target under six minutes. Every line names the key
pressed and what appears. The order never needs a restart.

**Status: stub — kept current as the build proceeds, finished at step 13.**

## Running order

1. **Boot from `file://`.** Double-click `game.html`. Title, then `NEW ROUND`.
   Play the first thirty seconds of course 1 — the ball, the descent, the fight with
   momentum.
2. **Two named systems visibly interacting.** Pair to be named once the hazard roster
   is placed and observed. Candidates from the spec: the rival marble rammed off the
   paper it was chasing you across (LAW 5.5 both ways), or the muncher pulling a ball
   off a belt that is pushing it the other way. Course and approximate place to be
   filled in from observation.
3. **A loss and its aftermath** — `BALL LOST`, the banner, the two-second hold with the
   hazards still cycling behind it, the respawn at the flag, the leg driven again, the
   clock carrying the cost.
4. **A checkpoint claimed** — the flag raising, the `-3.0s` receipt, the clock draining.
5. **Save, quit, reload.** Hole out to bank a course, `QUIT TO MENU`, close the browser
   tab entirely, reopen `game.html` from `file://`, `CONTINUE` restores the round — all
   through real UI, no debug key.
6. **`Ctrl+Shift+U`**, then the last course, then the round scorecard: the win state.
